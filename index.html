Our mission

Every player deserves pro-level feedback on their shot. ShotSync brings computer vision to the driveway and the school gym, so practicing alone still means getting better.

1

Record or upload

Prop up your phone facing the hoop, or upload a video you already filmed.

2

AI tracks the ball

A YOLO vision model finds the ball, rim and shooter in every frame. A make counts only when the ball goes down through the hoop.

3

Stats & coaching

Accuracy, streaks, a shot timeline and shot chart, plus short feedback from an AI coach.

4

Highlights, automatically

Every make is clipped and joined into a highlight reel, ready to share.
