ShotSync

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

ShotSync is a mobile app, developed by Weichang (Thomas) Yuan, that analyzes basketball practice videos. This policy explains what the app and its server collect, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

Information we collect

InformationWhy we use it
Account details: email address, password and the first name you enter. Guests get an anonymous account ID instead. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see them.To sign you in and keep your sessions linked to your account
Videos you record or choose, including the sound and anyone who appears in themTo detect shots and create your annotated video, clips, thumbnails and highlight reel
Session results: title, date, length, makes and misses, shot times, the shooter's estimated position in the video, starred clips and coaching feedbackTo show your stats, shot chart and progress
Technical data: IP address, request times and basic device or app information in server and service-provider logsTo run, secure and troubleshoot the service

The app has no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers. We do not collect your precise location or contacts, and we do not use face recognition.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and microphone: to record a session, only when you use the recording screen.
  • Photos or videos: to choose a video to analyze and to save your reel. The app uses only the video you choose, even if your device shows a broader access request.

You can turn permissions off in your device settings. Some features will then not work.

How videos are handled

When you record or choose a video, the app uploads it to our server for automated analysis. The server normally deletes the uploaded original as soon as analysis ends. It keeps the annotated video, highlight clips, thumbnails and reel for about 30 days so you can watch and share them.

If you film other people, please get their permission first (or a parent's or guardian's permission for a child).

Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. We use these providers to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and database): account details and session results. Firebase privacy
  • Amazon Web Services (server hosting): videos and technical data. AWS privacy
  • OpenAI (optional AI coach): only the session title and the numbers of makes, misses and attempts, and only after you allow it. You can turn it off in Settings. Your videos are not sent to OpenAI. OpenAI privacy
  • YouTube (Google): the Learn tab loads video thumbnails from YouTube, which receives your IP address. Tutorials open in YouTube under its own policy. Google privacy

If you share a reel or clip, the app or person you send it to receives it. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect people's safety. Our server and database are located in the United States.

Security

You must sign in to upload videos, and database rules limit session records to the account that created them. No system is completely secure, and you should know two current limits:

  • The connection between the app and our video server does not currently use HTTPS encryption, so avoid uploading on public Wi-Fi.
  • Video files are stored at long, random web addresses. Anyone who has the exact address of a file can open it.

How long we keep information

We automatically delete processed videos from our server about 30 days after they are made. We keep account details and session results (such as your stats) until you delete them or ask us to. Save a reel to your device if you want to keep it longer. Technical logs may be kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

Deleting your data or account

  • One session: open the session, tap the menu (⋮) and choose "Delete session." This deletes its results and its video files from our server.
  • Account and all data: on the Home tab, tap the gear icon, then "Delete Account." Email accounts enter their password; guests only confirm. This deletes your account, session results and video files from our server and cannot be undone.
  • If you cannot use the app, email weichang2026thomas@gmail.com with the subject "ShotSync data deletion," from the email address on your account. We will delete your data within 30 days and may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. For a guest account, include the date and approximate time of your sessions.
  • Guests: signing out or removing the app does not delete your sessions and may permanently remove your access to them. Use "Delete Account" first.

Videos you saved to your device or shared are not deleted. Technical logs may remain for a limited time, and we may keep limited information if the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to some uses. Email us to make a request. We will not treat you differently for doing so.

Children

ShotSync is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 should not create an account or upload videos. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before using the app. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
